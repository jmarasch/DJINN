# DJINN guide

How to run a party with DJINN, from installing it to fine-tuning what plays.
Each page covers one part of the app.

1. [Getting started](getting-started.md): install, create the admin account,
   add your music, and let guests in.
2. [Guests and requests](guests-and-requests.md): what guests see, how the
   queue works, backing songs, and skip votes.
3. [Playlists and radio](playlists-and-radio.md): what plays when nobody has
   requested anything.
4. [Smart play](smart-play.md): let the auto mix learn from the party, with a
   separate memory per kind of party.
5. [Rules](rules.md): guest mode, request limits, skip votes, and the other
   settings under Admin > Rules.
6. [Speakers and sound](speakers-and-sound.md): several speakers at once,
   delays, crossfade, and even volume.
7. [Casting, Play here and the TV page](casting-and-tv.md): Chromecast,
   smart TVs and Sonos, the party on your own phone, and a now-playing
   screen.
8. [Search and AI](search-and-ai.md): finding songs by name or by
   description, and the optional AI tools.
9. [Your library](your-library.md): music folders, artist photos and bios,
   correcting tags, and filling in missing ones.
10. [Updates](updates.md): keeping DJINN up to date.
11. [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): no sound, phones can't connect, and
    other common problems.

Who can do what: **guests** and **users** queue songs. **Admins** (hosts)
also control playback and see the Playlists and Admin tabs. The first account
you create is an admin.
